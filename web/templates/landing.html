Everything you'll actually use

  • {{icon "drop-left"}}

    Nursing that keeps up

    Tap left or right to start. The timer keeps counting even when your screen locks, and you can switch sides without stopping.

  • {{icon "clock"}}

    The last side, at a glance

    The top of the screen always shows which side and how long ago — so the next feed starts without the mental maths.

  • {{icon "diaper"}}

    Diapers in one tap

    Wet, dirty, or both. The time since the last one is always right there, ready for the next check-up question.

  • {{icon "moon"}}

    Sleep, gently tracked

    Start a nap and see how long your little one has been awake — before the overtired meltdown, not after.

  • {{icon "pencil"}}

    Easy to fix later

    Forgot to stop the timer? Add or take away five minutes, or edit the times. No starting over, no guilt.

  • {{icon "lock"}}

    Yours, and only yours

    Private to your household. No ads, no selling your data, and you can download every log whenever you like.

Quiet hours

Soft on tired eyes

At night the screen settles into a warm, low-glare palette. If a feed runs long, a soft ping and a gentle buzz let you know — repeating until you tap Done, never resetting when you switch sides.
