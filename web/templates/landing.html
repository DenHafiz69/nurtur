Fair questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?{{icon "chevron-down"}}
On this server, yes. An account keeps the log private to your household, and each account gets its own baby profile. Sign-ups may be invite-only — the app will tell you if you need a key.
Does it work offline?{{icon "chevron-down"}}
Nurtur installs to your home screen and caches its shell, so it opens without a connection. New logs wait for the connection to return instead of getting lost.
Can I fix a mistake?{{icon "chevron-down"}}
Every feed, diaper, and sleep can be edited or deleted. Deletions have an Undo, and the app checks the times so you cannot accidentally create two open feeds or overlapping sleeps.
Is Nurtur a medical device?{{icon "chevron-down"}}
No. Nurtur is a logbook. It does not diagnose, predict, or give medical advice. If something about your baby worries you, talk to your paediatrician or midwife — not an app.
