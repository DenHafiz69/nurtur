Fair questions

The things you're
probably wondering.

Is the free version really the whole tracker?
Yes. Nursing, diapers, sleep, the timeline, corrections, night mode, the locked-screen alert, widgets, two babies, unlimited local history, and export are all free. The free tier is not a trial, and it does not expire.
Do I need an account?
No. The app works on your phone with no sign-up, no email, and no server. You only need an account if you choose Plus for cloud backup, sync, or caregiver sharing.
What happens if I cancel Plus?
Sync pauses and the cloud copy stays readable for 90 days in case you change your mind. Everything on your phone keeps working. Export is always one tap away, and nothing is deleted just because a subscription ended.
Does the nursing alert work when the phone is locked?
That is the feature we are building the native app around. It uses an exact alarm and a high-importance notification, so it can fire while the screen is off and the app is closed. Android's battery settings and some phone makers can still interfere; the app will explain how to fix that rather than pretending it never happens.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The local database is the write path. Logging never waits for the network. If you use Plus, changes sync when you are back online, and conflicts are shown in plain language instead of silently picking a winner.
Where is my data stored?
On your phone, in the app's private database. If you enable Plus, an encrypted copy is stored in Nurtur Cloud. If you self-host the Nurtur server, it stays on your server. There is no third-party analytics or ad network in the middle.
Is Nurtur a medical device?
No. Nurtur is a logbook. It does not diagnose, predict, or give medical advice. If something about your baby worries you, talk to your paediatrician or midwife — not an app.
When is iOS?
Not soon. Android first is a deliberate choice, not a marketing line. The shared domain layer is being written so an iOS app can reuse the rules and the sync API later, but no date is promised until the Android app has earned it.
Can I share the log with my partner or a sitter?
Yes, with Plus. Invite up to five caregivers into one household — no per-seat charge — and everyone sees the same timeline. The free app stays on one phone with no account.
How much will it cost in my country?
Google Play sets local prices, and we will adjust the big markets rather than let a straight currency conversion produce something silly. The free app is free everywhere. The Plus price will be shown in your local currency before you buy.
